The site no longer renders any of these: src/lib/content.ts drops dead
<img>/<audio>/<figure> blocks at render time, so they show as nothing rather
than as broken images. Recovering a file is therefore optional — drop it into
public/media/ named <basename>-<sha1-of-original-url>.jpg and it comes back
automatically, or re-run:

  node scripts/migrate-images.mjs --download --from=~/Downloads/uploads --rewrite
