Images neither the WordPress origin nor the Jetpack CDN will serve.
Paths are relative to public_html/wp-content/uploads/ on the host.

Easiest route — let the script pull them over FTPS:
  FTP_HOST=<host> FTP_USER=<user> FTP_PASS=<pass> \
    node scripts/migrate-images.mjs --download --ftp --rewrite

Or download them by hand into public/media/ and re-run with --download --rewrite.
